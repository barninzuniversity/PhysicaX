Formulas Used

Ideal Gas Law

P V = n R T

Assumes ideal gas behavior, thermodynamic equilibrium, absolute temperature.

First Law

ΔU = Q - W

W = work done by system, Q = heat added to system.

Isothermal Work

W = n R T ln(V₂ / V₁)

Reversible ideal gas process.

Adiabatic Relation

P V^γ = const

Reversible adiabatic ideal gas. γ = Cp/Cv.
